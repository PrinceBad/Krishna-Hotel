About Us

Serving quality hospitality and delicious flavors since 2010

Experience True Hospitality

Krishna Hotel is a well-known local boutique hotel and restaurant located in Sector 48, Sonipat, Haryana. We have been welcoming our guests with comfortable accommodations and mouth-watering flavors for over a decade.

Our hotel offers clean, beautifully styled rooms with modern amenities at competitive prices. Our restaurant serves a rich menu of authentic North Indian, South Indian, and Chinese dishes prepared by master chefs using select ingredients.

Whether you are a traveler seeking peaceful accommodation or a family looking to enjoy an exceptional fine-dining dinner, Krishna Hotel welcomes you with absolute warmth.
